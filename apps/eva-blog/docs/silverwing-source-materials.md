## What The Design Model Should Extract

- Palette: frost white, silver gray, blue-lilac shadows, graphite black, cobalt/electric-blue accents, tiny warm rose/red accents only as signal.
- Materials: ceramic-white armor, matte dark technical fabric, translucent holographic blue panels, chrome/silver bevels, soft glow edges.
- Forms: wing/cape asymmetry, falcon-like tapered geometry, thin mechanical seams, floating panels, scatter-barrier planes, orbital/star-ring marks.
- Motion: quick but smooth state shifts, time-fracture freeze moments, light aerial hover, pulse/scan effects for summaries and auth status.
- Tone: mature, composed, precise, cool, future-tech elegance.

## What To Avoid

- Do not paste the character portrait into the blog UI.
- Do not make a collage of Honkai screenshots.
- Do not overuse neon blue glow.
- Do not turn the blog into a combat HUD.
- Do not add unsupported game features, fake analytics, reactions, or gacha-like panels.
- Do not sexualize or copy the exact outfit; extract the design grammar instead.
